Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and informs You about Your privacy rights and how applicable laws protect You.

We use Your Personal Data to provide, maintain, and improve the Service. We collect, use, and disclose Your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection legislation (including Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector / Law 25).

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

The words whose initial letters are capitalized have meanings defined under the following conditions. The following definitions shall have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or in plural.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

Collecting and Using Your Personal Information

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may receive certain personally identifiable information if You choose to contact Us voluntarily. This information may include:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Content of your inquiry or correspondence

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when accessing the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of Our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

Zero Cookies Policy: This website does not use any tracking cookies, third-party analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or profiling scripts. Your browsing on this site is entirely private and anonymous.

Local user preferences (such as light/dark mode selection) are processed entirely client-side using browser local storage without storing any identifiable personal data or sending information to any third-party server.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain Our Service, including monitoring the technical performance and security of the Website.
  • To contact You: To reply directly by email to your questions, media inquiries, or civic correspondence.
  • To manage Your requests: To process and follow up on any requests You submit to Us.

We do not sell, rent, or trade Your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With Service Providers: Strictly to host, maintain, and secure the Website infrastructure.
  • With Your Consent: For any other specific purpose where You have provided explicit consent.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company retains Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Correspondence & Inquiries: Retained for up to 24 months from the date of the message to handle follow-up exchanges or legal records.
  • Usage Data & Server Logs: Technical logs (such as IP addresses and access timestamps) are retained for up to 24 months strictly for security monitoring, fraud prevention, and server diagnostics.

Data is securely deleted or anonymized once retention periods expire.

Transfer of Your Personal Data

Your information is processed in Quebec, Canada, and by trusted hosting providers. If data is stored on servers located outside of Your jurisdiction, We ensure that adequate security controls and safeguards are in place to protect Your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Access, Correction, and Deletion of Your Data

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any Personal Data that You have provided to Us.

To exercise your privacy rights under Quebec's Law 25 or applicable privacy laws, please contact Us directly using the secure contact button below.

Disclosure of Your Personal Data

The Company may disclose Your Personal Data in good faith only if required to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation or valid court order.
  • Protect and defend the rights, property, or safety of the Company, visitors, or the public.
  • Prevent or investigate potential wrongdoing or security breaches related to the Service.

Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us. While no method of digital transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, We implement commercially reasonable technical safeguards to protect Your information.

Children's and Minors' Privacy

Our Website is an open educational and civic information platform. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental knowledge or guidance where required by applicable law. If You believe a child has provided personal information without consent, please contact Us and We will promptly remove the data.

Links to Other Websites

Our Service contains links to third-party websites (such as official registries, mapping tools, scientific dossiers, and media outlets). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify Users of any updates by posting the revised version on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
