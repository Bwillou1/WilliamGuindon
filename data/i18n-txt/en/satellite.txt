## Spectral Comparator Before / After (True colors vs. Water & Plant Analysis)

Drag the slider horizontally to overlay true color (RGB) imagery and spectral analysis highlighting
the water saturation of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog) facing the
Stablex industrial mineral enclave.

## Copernicus Program

The spectral bands B04 (Red), B03 (Green) and B02 (Blue) of Sentinel-2 L2A are processed for a
natural rendering calibrated with a reflectance coefficient optimized for the observation of plant
and humid strata.

## Ecological Integrity

The Grande Tourbière de Blainville constitutes the largest wooded wetland in the Lower Laurentians
and a major carbon reservoir. The industrial enclave in the center has been operated by Stablex
since 1983.
