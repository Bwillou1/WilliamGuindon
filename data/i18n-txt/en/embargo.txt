# Press Embargo & Media Relations
Fraternal practices, mutual respect of broadcast dates and open collaboration

## 1. Fraternal collaboration framework

This page presents the usual collaborative practices between William Guindon and news journalists.
It aims to promote the smooth transmission of documents of public interest, with mutual respect for
publication deadlines.

## 2. Simple embargo principles

- Definition: The embargo allows a press release or evidentiary document to be transmitted in advance
-   to allow journalists to study the file and prepare their coverage.
- Commitment of trust: When a deadline date and time are mentioned, the journalists undertake
-   fraternally not to publish before the agreed deadline.
- Clarity: Any transmission under embargo is expressly identified with its lifting time.

## 3. Interviews and direct collaboration

William directly receives all interview requests (virtual or face-to-face). In accordance with the
ethical rules applicable to minors, interviews are subject to customary parental consent.

To organize an interview or access media resources, go directly to the Official Press page ↗.

## 6. Selection of the press review and representativeness of the approaches

The press review presented on this site brings together a selection of reports, interviews and
surveys devoted to the Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog) file and
SEM-26-003 communication.

Note of non-exhaustiveness: This selection is not exhaustive. The absence of an article, report or
media on this page in no way constitutes a judgment on its credibility, independence or journalistic
work. This site primarily highlights publications which allow the public and researchers to trace
the evolution of the scientific and legal data in the case.

If your report or publication is rigorous, respects the facts and ethics, but does not appear in the
press review, it is very possible that it has simply escaped my attention (I am not glued to the
continuous news!). Please feel free to forward the link to me directly at contact@williamguindon.me
so that it can be reviewed and included in the official registry.

This document is a standalone policy of the site. Each media outlet retains its editorial
independence. No signature is required for its publication on the site.
