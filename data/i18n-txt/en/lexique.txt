# Lexicon & Citizen Dictionary
International law, water chemistry and parliamentary procedures often use complicated jargon that
discourages citizens. This page translates into plain language all the acronyms, legal articles,
scientific terms and official documents in the Stablex file and the SEM-26-003 communication, so
that everyone — whatever their age or background — can understand everything.

## ACEUM (CUSMA / USMCA)

It is the major commercial and legal treaty that binds Canada, the United States and Mexico since
2020 (replacing NAFTA). Its Chapter 24 prohibits the three countries from weakening or ignoring
their own environmental laws to favor a company.

## CCE (CEC)

Official international organization created by Canada, the United States and Mexico, headquartered
in Montreal. Its independent Secretariat reviews citizen complaints when one of the three states
fails to effectively enforce its environmental laws.

## SEM-26-003

This is the official file number given by CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) to the
legal complaint filed by William Guindon regarding the expansion of Stablex and the destruction of
Grande Tourbière (Great Peat Bog) de Blainville (“Landfill of hazardous materials in Blainville”).

## Article 24.27 of ACEUM

Rule of the ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement) treaty which allows any citizen to file a
complaint before the CCE (art. 24.27(1)), sets the criteria for validity (art. 24.27(2)) and allows
the Secretariat to oblige the government concerned to respond officially within 60 days (art.
24.27(3) and (4)).

## Article 24.28 of ACEUM

Once the government responds, Article 24.28 gives the CCE Secretariat 60 days to analyze the
response and decide whether to recommend to the Ministers of the Environment of the three countries
(Canada, United States, Mexico) the opening of a thorough investigation called a “Factual File”.

## Factual Record

This is the in-depth investigation report carried out by the scientific and legal experts of the
CCE. It is triggered if at least 2 of the 3 member countries vote for its opening. He publicly
exposes all the facts about non-compliance with environmental laws.

## Exhibit 72-RSP

Official code of the brief filed by the Government of Canada on October 16, 2026 before the CCE. In
this document, Ottawa attempts to close or postpone the SEM-26-003 file by invoking the existence of
a simple citizen petition to the Auditor General.

## Exhibit 73-REC

Official act by which the CCE Secretariat confirms having received Canada's response (72-RSP) on
October 16, 2026 and begins the 60-day countdown provided for in Article 24.28.

## Guideline 7.5 (SEM Procedures)

Official rule of CCE which specifies that a government can only request the cessation of a CCE
investigation if it itself conducts a real trial or a binding inspection capable of resolving the
problem, and not when a citizen has simply asked questions in a petition.

## Loi 93 (Projet de loi 93)

Special law adopted under gag order by the government of Quebec on March 28, 2025. It forces Ville
de Blainville (City of Blainville) to sell its wooded and humid land to the American company Stablex
and dismisses the unfavorable conclusions of BAPE (Québec Bureau of Public Environmental Hearings).

## Parliamentary gag order

Mechanism by which a majority government cuts short the debates of deputies and public consultations
at the Assemblée nationale (National Assembly of Québec) to have a law adopted in a few hours.

## BAPE (Report 371)

Independent public organization in Quebec responsible for informing and consulting citizens on major
projects. In its Report 371 published in September 2023, the BAPE concluded that the Stablex Cell 6
project in the peatland should not be permitted in its proposed form.

## BVG & CEDD

Independent institution of the federal Parliament in Ottawa which verifies the work of ministries
and manages the register of citizen environmental petitions.

## Environmental Petition No. 546 (Article 22 LVG)

Right provided by federal law allowing a citizen to ask formal questions to federal ministers about
the environment. Ministers have a legal obligation to respond in writing within 120 days, but this
mechanism does not provide the power to order work to stop.

## Cadmium (Cd)

Industrial metal dangerous to fish, wildlife and human health, which does not degrade in nature. In
a sample from the Ministry of the Environment (MELCCFP) taken on December 2, 2023 from the effluent
linked to Stablex activities, cadmium reached 320 times the acute toxicity threshold for aquatic
life.

## Cell 6 (Stablex)

This is the name given to the Stablex expansion project aimed at digging a huge landfill site for
millions of tons of hazardous waste treated until 2065, directly in the heart of a wooded and humid
sector of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog).

## Peat bog (and wetland)

A bog is a real natural sponge that has formed over thousands of years. It filters water, prevents
flooding, cools the air and shelters rare plants and animals. Unlike a tree plantation, a peat bog
destroyed by mechanical excavators cannot be recreated artificially.

## Carbon sink

Peatlands store proportionally much more carbon than forests. When a peatland is drained and dug,
the carbon trapped for centuries is released into the atmosphere in the form of greenhouse gases,
worsening climate change.

## Fisheries Act — Section 36(3)

Very powerful Canadian law, section 36(3) of which strictly prohibits the discharge of any toxic or
harmful substance into waters where fish live, or in any place from which this substance risks
flowing into these waters.

## SARA (SARA)

Federal law that protects endangered animal and plant species in Canada and their critical habitat,
with the possibility for the federal government to adopt a safety net or emergency order when the
province does not protect them.

## LCOM (MBCA)

Federal law resulting from an international treaty that prohibits destroying nests, eggs and
migratory birds or degrading protected areas during nesting.

## MELCCFP

This is the Quebec Ministry of the Environment. It was its own laboratory analyzes and internal
technical opinions which documented the severe cadmium exceedances and the very high ecological
value of the peat bog.

## ECCC & MPO

The two federal government departments in Ottawa responsible for enforcing the Fisheries Act
(pollution section), the Species at Risk Act (SARA) and the Migratory Birds Act (MBA).

## Forced expropriation

Normally used to build a school, a hospital or a public road, expropriation was here imposed by the
Loi 93 (Bill 93) to wrest from the Ville de Blainville natural land that it wanted to protect, in
order to transfer it for landfill purposes by a private company.

## Adultism & Civic Capacity

Adultism consists of underestimating or dismissing a person's speech, intelligence or legal actions
simply because they are under 18 years of age. However, the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child
(art. 12) guarantees the right of young people to be heard in decisions that affect their future.

## Leachate & Process water

When rain or groundwater comes into contact with buried or processed materials, it picks up heavy
metals (like cadmium) and other chemicals. This water must be rigorously treated before any
discharge.
