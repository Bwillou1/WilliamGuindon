# Registry & Hub of Integrity Policies and Charters
Exhaustive official directory of fundamental charters and legal documents governing citizen action
by William Guindon (SEM-26-003 CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) / ACEUM (Canada–United
States–Mexico Agreement)).

### Architecture of the Ethical and Legal Framework
In order to guarantee absolute clarity, flawless traceability and rigorous legal accessibility for
citizens, researchers, media and international bodies, the normative framework is split into
distinct and autonomous policies, supplemented by the legal conditions of use of the platform. Each
document has its own official page with its timestamp and delimited scope.

## The Fundamental Charters

### 01. Information Ethics & Media Rules
Commitments to documentary rigor, obligation of factual truth, ban on truncated quotes and protocol
of ethical requirements towards journalists.

### 02. Declaration of Independence & Integrity
Absolute political neutrality, non-partisan affiliation, self-financing of research, supervision of
conference fees, refusal of grants or partisan donations, and Formal public clarification notice
(A.5) on SEM-26-003.

### 03. AI Ethics & Human Control
Ethical supervision of AI models, exclusive human control over all background texts, integrity of
the training corpus, algorithmic transparency and formal legal exclusion of the AI ​​Documentary
Assistant.

### 04. Anti-SLAPP Statement & Legal Protection
Shield against SLAPP lawsuits (art. 51-54 C.P.C. Qc), right of international citizen participation
to the CCE (art. 24.27-24.28 ACEUM) and defense of environmental public interest debate.

### 05. Embargo Policy & Press Relations
Formal framework for early transmission of exclusive documents to accredited journalists and
sanctions in the event of embargo breaking.

### 06. Expert Consultative Framework & Scientific Rigor
Rules for occasional technical consultation with 16 scientists and lawyers, strict distinction
between consultative assistance and absence of co-signature.

### 07. Traceability of Evidence & Chain of Custody
Certified SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprints, immutable timestamp on the Bitcoin blockchain
(OpenTimestamps) and evidentiary custody chain.

### 08. Whistleblower Security (OpSec)
Operational security guidelines for secure and anonymous transmission of documents via Session,
Nostr and cryptographic channels.

### 09. Minor Citizen Status & Legal Guidelines
International citizen participation rights recognized for minors (United Nations Convention on the
Rights of the Child) and Quebec protective framework.

### 10. Privacy & Family Protection
Protection of the private sphere, family sanctuary and respect for autonomous communications by
William Guindon.

### 11. Accuracy & Media Relations Guidelines
Guidelines for factual integrity, transparency on non-exhaustive documentary selection and
preferences for equitable collaboration with information professionals.

### 12. Protection of the Published Documentary Archive
Evidentiary fixity, copyright compliance, legitimate reasons for redaction and formal withdrawal
procedure on Internet Archive and mirror platforms.

### 13. Correspondence, Archiving & Public Dissemination
Scope of correspondence (email, LinkedIn, Messenger), right to document capture and archiving,
commitment to complete anonymization of private exchanges and framework for public interest
dissemination of official communications.
