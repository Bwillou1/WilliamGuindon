## Who should I contact to report a situation?

For your report to be processed quickly and efficiently by sworn inspectors, it is essential to
choose the right level of intervention depending on the nature of the event:

### Emergency-Environment
Sudden events, ongoing chemical spills, acute toxic fumes or immediate danger to waterways.
- Leaking tanker truck or active spill
- Oil slick or chemical on water
- Persistent suffocating chemical odors

### Ministry of the Environment
Environmental violations noted, damage to wetlands or non-compliance with ministerial
authorizations.
- Unauthorized filling or drainage of bog
- Abnormal discharge of wastewater or suspicious coloring
- Illegal dumping of hazardous materials or contaminated soil
- Excavation work without certificate of authorization
- Abnormal mortality of fish or aquatic fauna

### Ville de Blainville
For problems that fall under the municipality and municipal planning regulations.
- Wild dumping of waste or bulky items
- Felling of trees or deforestation without municipal permit
- Local nuisances (noise outside of hours, dust)
- Obstructed ditches or blocked storm flow
- Cleanliness of marked public trails

## Methodical guide: How to document an observation

A well-founded report is based on verifiable, precise and reproducible facts. Follow these 8 steps
to build an impeccable file:
- Note the exact date and time: Indicate the exact start and end time of your observation.
- Note the exact location: Note the GPS coordinates (via your phone) or fixed visual landmarks
-   (terminal number, post, intersection, footbridge).
- Describe the phenomenon factually: Describe what you see, smell or hear without excessive
-   interpretation (e.g.: “Presence of an iridescent film on an area of ​​approximately 15 m²” rather
-   than “It’s polluted”).
- Take contextualized photographs: Combine a wide overview to identify locations with clear close-up
-   views.
- Record a short video: If the event involves movement (cloudy water flow, backflow, smoke emission),
-   a 15 to 30 second video provides convincing dynamic proof.
- Specify whether the event is active or completed: Clearly indicate whether the spill or work is in
-   progress at the time you submit the report.
- Strictly respect safety rules: Do not touch any product, do not walk on unstable ground and never
-   leave authorized public paths.
- Keep a dated copy of your shipment: Note the reference number of the file sent to the authorities
-   for citizen monitoring.

#### Fundamental safety & legality reminder
It is strictly forbidden to enter private property or fenced industrial sites. Never take liquid or
gas samples yourself without certified protective equipment. Your safety and respect for the law
take precedence over any observation.

## Don’ts: Security, legality & Protection of minors

### The 8 strict prohibitions
- Never enter private property or a fenced industrial area (violation of home/property).
- Never touch, inhale or taste any unknown substance, cloudy standing water or suspicious residue.
- Never take samples without certified equipment and compliant protocol.
- Never spread false alarms or unsubstantiated sensationalist claims.
- Never harass municipal, industrial or civil servants in the exercise of their duties.
- Do not degrade the environment to document (do not trample sphagnum moss off trail or pick rare
-   plants).
- Do not venture alone into isolated marshy areas without means of communication.
- Never publish photos of identifiable people without their prior written consent.

### Good citizen practices
- Stay on marked public trails and authorized observation decks.
- Document with composure and precision (dates, times, photos, GPS).
- Use the official forms from the Ministry and the City for traced processing.
- Respect professional secrecy and the presumption of legality until the facts are established.
- Show courtesy in all democratic questions.

### Official notice of protection for minors
If you are under 18 years old and wish to act to protect the environment: never go alone to an
observation site and do not participate in any field process without the explicit consent of your
parents or legal guardian.

## Frequently asked questions (Citizen FAQ)

Direct and verified answers to the most frequently asked questions about the protection of Grande
Tourbière and reporting procedures:

### What should I do if I notice suspicious drainage near a humid environment?
If the spill presents an immediate danger, a suffocating chemical odor or a large volume, contact
Urgence-Environnement immediately at 1 866 694-5454 (24/7). Take photos and videos from a safe
distance, note the exact time and GPS coordinates, without ever coming into direct contact with the
liquid.

### How do you know if a problem is the responsibility of the City or the Ministry of the Environment?
The Ville de Blainville (450 434-5200 / town planning: 450 434-5254) deals with local illegal
dumping, municipal tree felling, construction site nuisances and town planning regulations. The
Ministry of the Environment (MELCCFP) and Urgence-Environnement (1 866 694-5454) deal with toxic
discharges, the filling of wetlands, dangerous materials and violations of the Loi sur la qualité de
l'environnement (Environment Quality Act).

### Can I make an environmental report anonymously in Quebec?
Yes. The online reporting form of the Ministry of the Environment of Quebec and the
Urgence-Environnement telephone line allow reports to be transmitted confidentially or anonymously,
while receiving a file number for follow-up.

### How can I prove that a protected species lives in the bog?
Use the free iNaturalist app from permitted public trails. Take sharp photos with geotagging enabled
on your phone. The scientific community and experts validate the identification of the species
(sphagnum moss, salamanders, birds), constituting admissible and time-stamped proof.

### Why is the SEM-26-003 file handled by an international commission (CCE / ACEUM)?
The Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE), established by the Canada-United States-Mexico
Agreement (ACEUM, chapter 24), allows any citizen or NGO to submit a communication demonstrating
that a State party is failing to ensure the effective application of its environmental laws. The CCE
(Commission for Environmental Cooperation) deemed the communication SEM-26-003 admissible on August
17, 2026.

### What does Canada's response of October 16, 2026 (72-RSP) contain and what's next?
On October 16, 2026, the Government of Canada submitted its official 59-page response (72-RSP)
requesting the closure of SEM-26-003 under the pretext of the Blainville municipal appeal and
federal petition no. 0546 (BVG/CEDD). As of October 17, 2026, William Guindon sent his brief of
observations proving that no court examines the non-application of federal laws (LCOM, LEP, LCPE).
The CCE Secretariat now has 60 working days (art. 24.28 ACEUM) to recommend a factual record, while
federal ministers must respond to petition 0546 by November 10, 2026.

### Can a minor participate in environmental protection?
Yes, through citizen science (iNaturalist), learning, awareness and civic monitoring. However, any
action on the ground, public questioning or contact with the media must be supervised and authorized
by the parents or legal guardians in order to guarantee the safety and well-being of the young
person.

### How can I support William Guindon without donating money?
William Guindon applies a strict policy of independence and refuses any financial donations for the
SEM-26-003 register. You can help by sending form letters to elected officials, using iNaturalist on
public trails, or relaying verified official documents and briefs.
