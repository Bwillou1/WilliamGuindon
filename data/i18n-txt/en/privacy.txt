# Privacy Policy
Last legal update in effect: September 6, 2026 (September 6, 2026)

### Commitment to legal and ethical compliance
This Privacy Policy establishes in a transparent, factual and exhaustive manner the rules governing
the collection, use, retention, protection and destruction of personal information on the
williamguindon.me site. It is established in strict compliance with the Act respecting the
protection of personal information in the private sector of Quebec (CQLR, c. P-39.1, amended by Loi
25 (Québec privacy modernization Act)), the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act (PIPEDA / PIPEDA) of Canada, and aligns with the standards of excellence of the General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR) of the European Union.

private sector of Quebec (Loi 25), the person assuming the highest authority and legal
responsibility for the protection of personal information is:

## 2. Fundamentals of Processing and Zero Profiling

The site applies the cardinal principles of Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default:
- Absolute minimization: No personal data is collected without an explicit, legitimate and
-   proportionate purpose.
- No commercialization: Personal information is never sold, rented, exchanged, monetized or
-   transferred to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.
- Absence of automated decision-making: No profiling, discriminatory automated decision-making or
-   behavioral evaluation system is implemented on this site.

## 3. Data Collected and Processing Methods

The site does not install any advertising cookies, any cross-site trackers, any invisible pixels or
any commercial retargeting scripts.
- Local display preferences: The choice of display mode (dark or light theme) is stored exclusively in

-   on your device, is not associated with any personal identity and is never transmitted to our

- Privacy-friendly audience measurement (Umami Analytics): The site uses the open-source statistical
-   analysis solution Umami Analytics (hosted on cloud.umami.is). Unlike invasive analysis tools,
-   Umami works without any connection cookies (cookieless), does not collect any data allowing the
-   identification of an individual (PII), completely anonymizes IP addresses by daily cryptographic
-   hashing with salt, and does not track any user from one site to another. This mechanism strictly
-   complies with the directives of the CNIL, the Loi 25 and the GDPR and does not require any

## 4. Secure Messaging, Client-Side Encryption and Source Protection (Nostr / NIP-59 / NIP-44)

As part of the protection of journalistic sources, whistleblowers and engaged citizens, the site
integrates a cutting-edge communication module based on the decentralized Nostr (Secure Messaging)
protocol governed by the following technical guarantees:
- Zero-Knowledge Architecture: All cryptographic operations are executed exclusively on the client
-   side (in the RAM of your browser) via the standardized Web Crypto API. No private keys, no clear
-   messages and no decryption keys pass through or are hosted on williamguindon.me servers.
- Triple Cryptographic Envelope (NIP-59 Gift Wrap & NIP-17): The initial message (*Rumor*, kind 14) is
-   encrypted and sealed (*Seal*, kind 13), then inserted into a disposable wrapper (*Gift Wrap*, kind
-   1059) signed by a unique random ephemeral key. Decentralized network relays cannot technically
-   know the identity of the sender, the recipient, or the actual transmission timestamp.
- NIP-44 v2 encryption and protection against traffic analysis: The encryption combines ECDH on
-   secp256k1 curve, HKDF-SHA256, AES-GCM 256 bits and a geometric padding algorithm, preventing any
-   spying by analyzing the size of network packets.
- Encrypted files and attachments: Any attached document or photograph is encrypted locally in RAM in
-   AES-GCM 256 bits before distribution. The symmetric decryption key is sealed inside the encrypted
-   envelope NIP-59.
- Ephemerality and irreversible purge (NIP-40): The user can assign a programmable self-destruction
-   duration to his message, ordering its definitive deletion from all network relays.
- Right to immediate deletion on the device: A complete purge button (“Erase everything from this
-   device”) allows the user to instantly destroy their local keys in RAM memory, their caches and any
-   trace of session.
- Total absence of obligation and full discharge: Sending an encrypted message does not confer any
-   mandate, establishes any professional or legal relationship, and in no way commits William Guindon
-   to respond, publish, disclose or process the transmitted elements. The sender remains solely
-   responsible for his transmissions and the safekeeping of his private keys.

### 4.1. Secure Transmission of Large Files and Voluminous Folders (SwissTransfer / Infomaniak)
For sending evidentiary documents, technical archives or recordings exceeding the limit of email
attachments (up to 50 GB per transfer, free and without registration), the SwissTransfer service
(operated by Infomaniak Network SA in Switzerland) is recommended under the following legal and
technical guarantees:
- 1. Intellectual property and rights to data: Infomaniak does not grant itself any rights to your
-   files. The content is never analyzed, never resold, and never used to train artificial
-   intelligence models.
- 2. Ephemerality and permanent deletion: The files are stored strictly temporarily (up to 30 days
-   maximum at the sender's choice) and are permanently and irreversibly deleted from the servers as
-   soon as the link expires or after downloading.
- 3. Double encryption in transit and at rest: Transfers circulate via an encrypted HTTPS / AES-GCM
-   protocol (secure anti-interception tunnel) and benefit from double hardware and software locking
-   at rest (LUKS and AES 256 bits). The sender can also lock access with a personalized password.
- 4. Swiss sovereignty and protective legal framework: The infrastructure is 100% Swiss, operated in
-   Infomaniak's proprietary data centers, without any subcontracting to American hyperscalers. The
-   data is governed by the Federal Data Protection Act (Swiss DPA) and the European GDPR. In the
-   absence of an American parent company, the infrastructure is not subject to direct
-   extraterritorial injunction mechanisms such as the American CLOUD Act.
- Technical transparency: Encryption keys are managed server-side to enable direct web distribution
-   without requiring the installation of third-party software at the recipient.

## 5. AI Virtual Assistance Module (Cloudflare Workers & Groq)

The site provides a citizen conversational assistant to facilitate access and understanding of
public environmental documentation (folder CCE SEM-26-003). The processing is governed by the
following rules:
- Secure Serverless Proxy (Cloudflare Workers): Requests to the helper are routed through a serverless
-   function (Serverless Edge Worker) running on Cloudflare's edge network. This proxy completely
-   isolates the inference secret key, filters requests, and enforces strict guardrails without ever
-   storing content on a persistent server.
- Transient and anonymous processing: The questions asked and a contextual extract from the public
-   documentation are transmitted in real time by the Worker to the Groq inference engine. No user
-   account, cookie or personal identifier is required or transmitted.
- Persistent lack of logging: The site editor does not keep any logs of conversations, any chat
-   history or any log of prompts submitted to the assistant.
- Non-use for training: The data from your queries is in no way used by the publisher to train or
-   refine artificial intelligence models.
- Absence of automated decision (Loi 25, art. 12.1): The assistance module is a purely informative and
-   documentary tool. It does not render any automated decision producing legal effects or affecting
-   in any way the persons concerned.
- Technical retention of the Groq processor: Requests passing through the Groq Inc. API are processed
-   in RAM. In accordance with Groq's corporate policy for its API inference services, data is
-   retained for a maximum technical period of 30 days for security and abuse detection purposes only,
-   with no reuse for model training.
- No Advice and Disclaimer: AI responses are generated probabilistically and automatically. They do
-   not in any way constitute a promise, legal advice, expert advice or a formal position taken by the
-   publisher.
- Caution: Users are strongly recommended not to enter any confidential or sensitive personal
-   information into the AI ​​chat interface.

## 8. Rights of Data Subjects (Loi 25, PIPEDA, GDPR)

Under the legislation applicable in Quebec and international data protection standards, you have the

- Right to withdraw consent: Right to withdraw your consent to the processing of your data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, you may send a written request to the Privacy Officer (William

## 9. Data Security and Protection Measures

Rigorous physical, organizational and technological security measures are deployed to preserve the
integrity, confidentiality and availability of your data:
- Mandatory end-to-end TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption protocol with enhanced HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport
-   Security) preload policy;
- Delivery via Cloudflare secure edge network with application firewall (WAF) and active DDoS
-   protection;
- Restrictive Content Security Policy (CSP): whitelist of authorized domains, prohibition of embedded
-   objects (object-src 'none'), prohibition of external framing (frame-ancestors) and script-src
-   restricted to trusted domains. Dynamic code evaluation disabled on PDF reader side (
-   isEvalSupported: false );
- Protection against clickjacking via HTTP headers X-Frame-Options: DENY and frame-ancestors 'none'
-   served at the edge;
- Partitioning of hardware permissions (default deactivation of the camera, microphone, geolocation
-   and USB devices via Permissions-Policy);
- Zero Trust protection hermetically isolates administration interfaces without local storage of
-   master passwords.
