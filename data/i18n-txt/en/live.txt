# Factual File: Deadline CCE of 60 Days
Official real-time count down to the second of the 60-day deadline given to the Commission de
coopération environnementale (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) Secretariat to determine
whether it recommends to the Council the establishment of an independent Factual Record (Art. 24.28
of ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement)), following the submission of Canada's official
response on October 16, 2026.

## Mandatory ministerial response · Petition 0546 (Art. 22 LVG)

Mandatory legal deadline of 120 days imposed on the ministers of ECCC and DFO to table their
official responses before the Parliament of Canada.

## Bilateral parliamentary meetings (Provincial & Federal)

Two official meetings in November 2026 to bring the SEM-26-003 file, the BAPE 371 and the federal
response to the BVG:
- Nadine Le Gal (Provincial Member of Blainville) — meeting scheduled even before the opening of her
-   permanent office.
- Madeleine Chenette (Federal Member of Parliament for Thérèse-De Blainville, Parliament of Canada).

## Submission of the federal response and examination by the Secretariat of the CCE

The Government of Canada formally submitted its official response (72-RSP) on October 16, 2026. The
CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) Secretariat is currently in the review phase governed
by section 24.28 of ACEUM to determine whether it recommends to the Ministerial Council the
development of an independent Factual Record.
