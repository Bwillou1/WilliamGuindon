# Public Appearances & Media Coverage
Major national investigative reports, television interviews, columns and citizen speeches
documenting the efforts of William Guindon, the first minor author in the history of the CCE
(SEM-26-003).
> “Devoting more than 250 hours to documenting such a complex environmental issue, at just 14 years
> old, demonstrates remarkable determination. Your work deserves to be considered with attention and
> respect. » — Official institutional correspondence · File SEM-26-003

## Blainville peat bog: soon to be buried under waste?

Large investigative file from the Agence Science-Presse devoted to the ecological and
hydrogeological risks of the expansion of the Stablex site. The article analyzes toxic cadmium
releases, the value of boreal wetlands and the international citizen procedure SEM-26-003 initiated
before the CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation).

## Stablex debate returns to Blainville City Hall

Regional investigative report in English from The North Shore News covering the debates at the
Blainville municipal council, the concerns of local residents and citizen interventions relating to
the challenge to Cell 6 of Stablex.

## Protection of a peat bog in Blainville: A teenager’s fight at the UN

“At the age of 14, William Guindon contacted the United Nations Special Rapporteur on Toxic
Substances to denounce the expansion of Stablex in the Grande Tourbière (Great Peat Bog). » Major
national report by Valérie Simard highlighting the rigor of her 250 hours of investigation.

## TVBL interview — Environmental preservation and Stablex file

Large 12-minute studio television interview given to Télévision des Basses-Laurentides (TVBL).
William Guindon dissects the legal foundations of the CCE SEM-26-003 submission, the risks of
cadmium and the mobilization for the Grande Tourbière.

## Face to face at the Municipal Council: Mortality at Lake Fauvel & Public Ethics

Official citizen intervention by William Guindon questioning Mayor Liza Poulin on the absence of
scientific analyzes justifying the municipal position regarding the Stablex file. Full certified
transcription available.

## “François Legault, you are destroying our future”

“Impacting open letter addressed to the Prime Minister of Quebec the day after the adoption under a
gag order of Projet de loi 93 (Bill 93) facilitating the expansion of the site. »Significant citizen
column by William Guindon.

## CBC Podcasts: This Is Montreal — The Fight for Blainville Peatland

Episode of the CBC News English-speaking metropolitan investigative series devoted to the protection
of wetlands in the Lower Laurentians and the international scope of the SEM-26-003 file.

## “Blainville teenager takes Stablex fight international”

Independent investigation by Taylor C. Noakes reporting on the international legal process initiated
by William Guindon before the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE) created under the
ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement).

## FCQGED — “International efforts towards Stablex continue”

“We were informed that, on August 17, 2026, the CCE rendered a decision attesting to the conformity
of the communication issued by citizen William Guindon. » Analysis of the Quebec Common Front for
Ecological Waste Management on the admissibility of the submission SEM-26-003 under ACEUM and the
deadline for Canada's response to October 16, 2026.

## “File an international complaint to enforce environmental laws”

Legal news article from Éducaloi popularizing the international citizen recourse under articles
24.27 and 24.28 of the ACEUM, citing the submission SEM-26-003 filed by William Guindon.

## “Super William to the rescue of the Grande Tourbière! »

Climate Aces section: youth report explaining the importance of preserving Grande Tourbière de
Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog) and saluting William's exceptional and persevering
commitment.

## “François Legault congratulates the first vaccinated child”

Historical archive of the public meeting with Quebec Prime Minister François Legault during the
launch of the youth vaccination campaign at the Palais des congrès de Montréal.

## ACTES CSQ Network Conference — Environmental education

Intervention and workshop presented as part of the ACTES Movement national conference on the rigor
of citizen research, mastery of legal texts and international evidentiary methodology.

## AREQ-CSQ Laurentides Green Day — Mobilization for the Peatland

Honorary plenary conference in front of retired members of education in the Laurentians on
intergenerational dialogue and mobilization for the preservation of Grande Tourbière de Blainville.

## Curium Mag — Large format interview: Scientific rigor and the law in the face of toxic waste

Exclusive interview already filmed and recorded for the Quebec science and society magazine for
14–17 year olds. William dissects the evidentiary approach of citizen communication SEM-26-003, mass
spectrometry, critical cadmium exceedances and the protection of biodiversity of Grande Tourbière.

## Lab22 (Earth Day) — Documentary project on the school ecological transition

Participation and documentary filming with the innovation laboratory for ecological transition in
secondary schools (Lab22). Highlighting independent citizen research, democratic perseverance and
mobilization for wetlands.
