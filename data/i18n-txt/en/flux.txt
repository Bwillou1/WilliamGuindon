### Canada's official response (SEM-26-003): The federal government is attempting a procedural rejection in the face of evidence of contamination of Grande Tourbière
The Government of Canada submitted its official response (72-RSP) to the Commission de coopération
environnementale (CCE) on October 16, 2026. He requests the rejection or postponement by invoking
environmental petition no. 0546 to the BVG, the ministerial response of which is due on November 10,
2026, without contesting the critical discharges of cadmium (320x the standard) nor the encroachment
on 278,000 m² of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville. The CCE has acknowledged receipt (73-REC) and
opens the examination under article 24.28 of the ACEUM.
