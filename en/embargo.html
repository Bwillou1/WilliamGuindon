Press Embargo & Media Relations

Fraternal practices, mutual respect of broadcast dates and open collaboration

Document d'information Collaboration médias · William Guindon (15 ans)

1. Fraternal collaboration framework

This page presents the usual collaborative practices between William Guindon and news journalists. It aims to promote the smooth transmission of documents of public interest, with mutual respect for publication deadlines.

2. Simple embargo principles

  • Definition : The embargo allows a press release or evidentiary document to be transmitted in advance to allow journalists to study the file and prepare their coverage.
  • Commitment of trust : When a deadline date and time are mentioned, the journalists undertake fraternally not to publish before the agreed deadline.
  • Clarity : Any transmission under embargo is expressly identified with its lifting time.

3. Interviews and direct collaboration

William directly receives all interview requests (virtual or face-to-face). In accordance with the ethical rules applicable to minors, interviews are subject to customary parental consent.

To organize an interview or access media resources, go directly to the Official Press page ↗. ↗
