Permanent Deactivation of the Nostr Web Testbed

This module was originally developed for technical experimentation and feasibility testing (Proof of Concept) around the decentralized Nostr protocol (NIP-59 / NIP-44).

In hindsight, this mechanism proved unnecessary and carried avoidable risks: embedding a direct anonymous dropbox and decryptor in a web browser unnecessarily exposes the site to spam vectors, unsolicited files, and security risks tied to executing cryptographic keys in a static page.

Par rigueur et principe de précaution, all network logic, WebSocket listeners, and in-browser encryption have been permanently removed from the source code. Les canaux éprouvés ci-dessus (Courriel et Session) répondent parfaitement aux normes les plus strictes de confidentialité sans faire courir le moindre risque d'hébergement ou d'exposition au site.
