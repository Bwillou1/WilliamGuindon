03 · Peuples

Several nations, the same country of water.

Two errors respond to each other. Assign the bog to a single people. Or start the story in 1683, with a lordship. A third would be to invent an oral story that no public source contains.

What “unassigned” means here For the island of Montreal and the south of the Laurentians, there is no numbered treaty by which a nation ceded its title to the Crown, as in part of the West. The French lordships were granted without the consent that contemporary law would recognize. Kanien’kehá:ka traditional authorities maintain that their traditional territory was never ceded. The press, for the Kahnawà:ke nation, describes it as extending from the St. Lawrence Valley to the Adirondacks and northern New York State, including the Montreal region. McGill University, in its territorial recognition, names the Kanien’kehá:ka guardians of the lands and waters of Tiohtià:ke. An educational document from UQTR places the south of the Laurentians in the Kanien’kehá:ka ancestral territory, and the Weskarini, an Anishinabe group, further north, in the Hautes-Laurentides.

Distinction fondamentale : La Maison Longue (Gouvernance traditionnelle) vs Le système de bande

A major political and legal distinction separates the Longhouse (traditional sovereign governance, carried by the Clan Mothers, hereditary chiefs and the Great Law of Peace / Kaianerehkó:wa) from the system of band councils imposed by the British Crown and the Indian Act to administer the colonial regime. Bodies such as band councils or the Assembly of First Nations (AFN) are state creations which in no way replace the inherent sovereignty of the peoples of the Long House (Kanonsonnkweonkwe). To understand the political significance of this terminological and institutional distinction, see the webinar on traditional sovereignty and Kannosonhkwe'ón:we ↗.

Perspectives d'études écologiques

The margins of the peat bog — drier cords, terraces of the Rivière aux Chiens, approaches to the Rivière des Mille Îles — represent major ecological transition zones. The heart of the peat complex constitutes a valuable paleoenvironmental archive: ancient pollens, sediments and traces of past ecosystems. The detailed analysis of pollen cores in Blainville represents an open area of ​​scientific research to better understand the plant and climatic history of the Lower Laurentians.

What the number “742 species” cannot carry A citizen mobilization in April 2025 claims that 742 species have been listed in the cell 6 sector. The figure is circulating. There is no official total in the ministerial documents cited by the press. The solid public totals are other: nearly 200 birds in the sector, 132 on the site in a report, nine fish mentioned in the sector, three species named in the catch correction, 97 hectares and more than 100 birds at Fauvel lakes, 92 species with status in the riverine refuge. A broad citizen inventory can be true without being quotable as a state figure. To reject it out of contempt, or to engrave it as truth, are two lazinesses.

Why is this living being “from the south” and “from the north” at the same time? The peat bogs of the alluvial plain, like those of Lanoraie further east on the north shore, are refuges: they keep northern plants (dwarf birch, low willows) and southern plants (Virginia woodwardie) far from the heart of their area. Blainville is in the same natural province. This is why a suburban peat bog can contain flora that you would think were boreal, next to a highway. The comparison with the Tourbières-de-Lanoraie ecological reserve is the best mirror available: same family of environments, real protection there, industrial file here.
