## Bilateral meeting with Nadine Le Gal, Member of Parliament for Blainville, and Sylvain Pagé

On November 6, 2026, William Guindon held an in-depth bilateral working session with the new MP for
the Blainville constituency in Assemblée nationale du Québec (National Assembly of Québec), Nadine
Le Gal, and Sylvain Pagé. Showing a keen sense of listening to citizens, the MP held this exchange
even before having opened her permanent constituency office.

### Delegation & Participants at the working table

### The 5 major advances and established directions
Nadine Le Gal formally reaffirmed that the safeguarding of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great
Blainville Peat Bog) and the critical examination of Stablex landfill activities constitute the
number one priority of her constituency mandate. The MP demonstrated rigorous listening to the 222
evidentiary documents placed in the public register.

At the heart of the regulatory and economic proposals, the delegation presented the establishment of
a dissuasive eco-fee of $1,000 per tonne on the burial of industrial toxic waste. This measure aims
to put an end to Blainville's status as a low-cost dump, discourage cross-border transfers and force
decontamination at source.

The discussions made it possible to re-examine the democratic injustice of the Quebec Loi 93 (Bill
93), imposed under parliamentary gag order to force municipal expropriation against the unanimous
verdict of report BAPE 371. The MP was also very critical of the federal government's posture at CCE
(Commission for Environmental Cooperation), which refuses to respond on cadmium (320x the standard)
to hide behind a procedural loophole.

The meeting resulted in specific operational steps:  parliamentary initiatives planned in December
2026 in synchronization with the international deadline of the CCE, the establishment of an Advisory
Council / permanent monitoring committee in January 2027, and the upcoming convening of an expanded
bilateral working session.

The MP praised the autonomy and rigor of the next generation of citizens and confirmed her wish to
involve young people in territorial decisions. The presence at the table of Édith Mora Castelan,
citizen expert from the First Nations and watchdog in residual materials management, reaffirmed the
imperative to fully integrate the representatives of the Kanien'kehá:ka Nation and respect for
ancestral rights in any lasting approach for the peat bog basin.

### Meeting with Madeleine Chenette, federal deputy of Thérèse-De Blainville
Parliamentary follow-up meeting scheduled for November 2026 with the federal MP representing the
constituency in the House of Commons of Canada. The discussions will focus on the legal deadline of
November 10, 2026 (mandatory ministerial response to petition 546 from the OAG) and Canada's
cooperation under article 24.28 of the ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement) before the
Secretariat of the CCE.

## Multilateral Dialogue & International Representations

The citizen diplomacy exercised by William Guindon transcends local borders to address the
monitoring bodies of international environmental and human rights treaties:

#### Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE)
Submission of communication SEM-26-003, positive admissibility under art. 24.27(2)(3) and legal
response refuting Canada's inadmissibility.

#### United Nations (Special Rapporteur)
Urgent appeal and citizen brief transmitted to Dr. Marcos A. Orellana, UN Special Rapporteur on
toxic substances and human rights.

#### Environmental Commissioner (BVG)
Public Audit Petition No. 0546 filed with Jerry DeMarco under Section 22 of the Auditor General Act.
