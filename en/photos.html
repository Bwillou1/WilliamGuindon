Ancien (entrepôt) de munitions au phosphore — Plan-Bouchard
Plan-Bouchard, Blainville · 2026-09-12 Download

Fence marking access to what appears to be a former National Defense sector at Plan-Bouchard. The site remains inaccessible (padlocks sealed with wax), although a breach is visible on the other side of the perimeter. The area could still harbor white phosphorous munitions residue. No hydrological impact study seems to have assessed whether the pumping envisaged in cell 6 risks lowering the local water table, while white phosphorus ignites spontaneously upon simple contact with air when it is exposed.
