Petition to the House of Commons in Parliament Assembled

Whereas:

Whereas section 15(1) of the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms guarantees to every individual equal protection and equal benefit of the law without discrimination based on age;
Whereas Article 12 of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, ratified by Canada, mandates that the views of the capable child be heard and given due weight in accordance with age and maturity in all administrative proceedings;
Whereas the Civil Code of Québec enables minors aged 14 and older to consent alone to required medical care, under the conditions of sections 14 and 17;
Whereas Canada currently possesses no federal statutory framework nor an independent commissioner to protect youth against administrative and institutional paternalism;
Whereas presuming by default the incapacity of youth aged 14 to 17 in their citizen and administrative proceedings violates their intellectual integrity and may constitute systemic discrimination.

We, the undersigned, Citizens and residents of Canada, call upon the House of Commons in Parliament assembled to request that the federal Government:

  1. Adopt, within its constitutional spheres of jurisdiction, a framework law against ageism and paternalism across federal public and private institutions and services, and negotiate a pan-Canadian intergovernmental accord;
  2. Create an independent Federal Youth Commissioner (ombudsperson), directly accessible to young people, tasked with receiving complaints and investigating federal institutions;
  3. Establish, for youth aged 14 to 17, a rebuttable default presumption of capacity to participate in administrative proceedings concerning them, revocable solely following an individualized assessment of age, maturity, and competence, never on age alone, accompanied by written grounds, respect for autonomy, and the right to be heard.
