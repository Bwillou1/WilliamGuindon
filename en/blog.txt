# News & Logbook
Official articles, legal analyzes and citizen reflections by William Guindon published directly
online.

## Bilateral meeting with Nadine Le Gal (MP for Blainville): Eco-royalty of $1,000/tonne, Loi 93 and citizen action calendar

On November 6, 2026, William Guindon held an official bilateral meeting with Nadine Le Gal, Member
of Parliament for Blainville, and Sylvain Pagé, alongside scientist Daniel Green and Édith Mora
Castelan (first nations citizen expert, field watchdog in residual materials management and
environmental rights). Absolute priority given to Grande Tourbière (Great Peat Bog), eco-royalty
project of $1,000/t, denunciation of federal inertia, creation of a council in January 2027 and
inclusion of First Nations.

## Documentary investigation: What the Official Registry of Lobbyists of Canada reveals during full examination of the file SEM-26-003

While Environment and Climate Change Canada left 10 citizen questions on cadmium unanswered for 119
days, an official meeting was held between the associate assistant deputy minister and the Stablex
representative on the subject of cell 6.

## Parliamentary meetings in November: Nadine Le Gal (MP for Blainville) and Madeleine Chenette (federal MP) listening to the citizen file

Two high-level meetings are confirmed for November 2026 with the region's parliamentary
representatives: Nadine Le Gal, new provincial MP for Blainville (who was keen to plan this exchange
even before the official opening of her constituency office), and Madeleine Chenette, federal MP for
Thérèse-De Blainville in the House of Commons of Canada.

## Canada's official response (SEM-26-003): The federal government is attempting a procedural rejection in the face of evidence of contamination of Grande Tourbière

In-depth analysis of the formal response (72-RSP) filed on October 16, 2026 by Canada before the CCE
(Commission for Environmental Cooperation): request for rejection or postponement invoking the
petition to the BVG, total absence of dispute over the releases of cadmium (320x) and the
destruction of the peat bog, and opening of the CCE examination under article 24.28 of the ACEUM
(Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement).

## Field reporting: Wildlife incidents and illegal dumping in the Plan-Bouchard sector

As part of field monitoring and ongoing documentation of local natural environments in Blainville, a
formal report was made concerning recent wildlife incidents as well as the presence of wild deposits
observed in the Plan-Bouchard woodland sector.

## Environmental Petition No. 546: Official registration with the Auditor General of Canada (CEDD)

Official registration of environmental petition no. 15-546 in the catalog of the Office of the
Auditor General of Canada (Commissioner of the Environment and Sustainable Development - CEDD),
questioning ECCC, DFO and Health Canada on the application of federal laws in Blainville.

## SEM-26-003 explained simply: The complete and accessible file on Grande Tourbière de Blainville

Publication of a large, popularized documentary summary in 12 parts simply explaining the history,
the allegations, the CCE decision of August 17, 2026, the actors and the numerical traps.

## Large documentary file: The peat bog that remembers — Indigenous history, ecology & water territories

Publication of a major independent documentary synthesis exploring 6,000 years of memory of the
Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog), the history of indigenous peoples
(Kanien'kehá:ka, Anishinaabeg, Haudenosaunee) and the water territories of the Mille Îles River
(Makigôtekw).

## Official confirmation of the CCE: First minor author in the history of the SEM mechanism since 1994

The Secretariat of the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE / ACEUM) formally certifies
in writing that William Guindon is the very first minor in 32 years of history (since 1994) to file
a citizen communication.

## Public clarification notice: Lack of collaboration and strict independence

Official clarification from William Guindon recalling the complete independence and the strictly
individual and self-financed nature of the file SEM-26-003 submitted to CCE, dispelling any
allegation of partisan aid or external co-editing.

## Advocating for the legal autonomy of adolescents: breaking the glass ceiling of institutional ageism

By defending complex environmental issues, William Guindon highlights the blind spot of bureaucratic
paternalism and institutional ageism that deprives adolescents aged 14 to 17 of their full civic and
legal capacity.

## Official launch of the search engine for supporting documents and the ZIP archive (96%+ of the file)

Uploading of the internal full-text search engine (OCR) of all the supporting documents of the
SEM-26-003 file, accompanied by a downloadable ZIP archive of more than 96% of the supporting
documents.

## Citizen Survey: Position of political parties on Stablex (Cell 6) and the decision CCE SEM-26-003

Methodology of the citizen survey and collection of positions of provincial political groups (CAQ,
PLQ, PQ, QS, PCQ, Climat Québec) on the Stablex file and the CCE ordinance.

## Behind the scenes of the filming with Lab22 at Lac Fauvel: raising the citizen voice

Return to a day of filming at Lac Fauvel with the Lab22 team, the opportunity to approach from a
more human and personal angle the motivations behind my civic submission to the CCE.

## Historic victory: CCE Secretariat orders official response from Canada

Return to the positive determination of August 17, 2026 made by Commission de coopération
environnementale (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) in file SEM-26-003.

## Submission of the revised 15-page submission to the Commission

Finalization and official submission of the consolidated version of the citizen submission
SEM-26-003 integrating scientific and legal data.
