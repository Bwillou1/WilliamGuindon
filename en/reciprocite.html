Transmission ciblée / Note officielle
Principe de réciprocité démocratique :
For months, citizen Access to Information (ATIP) requests seeking to document cadmium contamination, expropriation under Bill 93, and threats to 278,000 m² of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville met with systematic refusals, unreasonable delays, and hundreds of blacked-out pages. In its official October 16, 2026 response, Ottawa attempted a procedural dismissal to avoid answering on the substance of the environmental harm.

By virtue of this strict reciprocity, the accelerated summaries, internal documentary explorers and investigative files on this portal are reserved for investigative journalists, university researchers and citizens.

  • Obligatory official route: All of the 222 supporting documents and the author's observations are officially posted and can be consulted in the public register of the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE / ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement)) under the communication SEM-26-003.
  • No informal transmission: Representatives of ECCC, CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) and Republic Services / Stablex must follow the formal channels provided for in Article 24.28 of the international agreement.
