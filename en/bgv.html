Environmental Petition No. 0546 (OAG / CESD) · CEC Submission SEM-26-003

Environmental Petition 0546: The Federal Responses Have Arrived

Decisive Update — Official CEC Notification of Nov 25, 2026 (Doc. 89/PP): In its 12-page Notification (A24.27(2)(3)/SEM/26-003/89/PP ↗), the CEC Secretariat reproduces all 24 questions below in Table 2 (pp. 10–11) and rules that OAG Petition No. 0546 is not a pending proceeding, has already concluded, and cannot terminate SEM-26-003 — ordering overdue Canada to file its merits response without delay.

24 questions on Stablex, the Grande Tourbière de Blainville and the enforcement of federal environmental laws.

On November 10, 2026, I received the responses from Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) and Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO / MPO) to my environmental petition No. 0546.

I had asked questions regarding inspections, public reports, water and sediment sampling, migratory birds, species at risk and fish habitat in the Stablex sector.

I transmitted these responses to the Secretariat of the Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC), together with my supplementary observations in file SEM-26-003.

2. Key Facts

Fact 1 · Reports to ECCC

ECCC reports 16 complaints or reports received by its National Environmental Emergencies Centre between March 3 and June 14, 2025, four complaints received by EnviroInfo on June 14, 2025, and a Stablex report regarding a sediment release reported on June 18, 2026. The documents do not establish whether all reports came from 20 distinct individuals.

Fact 2 · Water & Sediment Sampling

ECCC states that it collected and analyzed water samples (April 9, 2025 and June 17, 2026) and sediment samples (June 17, 2026) in its laboratories. Numerical laboratory values are not included in its response.

Fact 3 · Fish Habitat & Connectivity

DFO recognizes that certain ditches and wetlands within Cell 6 constitute fish habitat and describes their hydrological connectivity with an unnamed watercourse southeast of the site, Locke Head Creek and the receiving network during spring floods and heavy rainfall.

Fact 4 · Emergency Authorization

DFO states that it received an application on April 28, 2026 and issued an emergency authorization on May 29, 2026, subject to conditions, for Phase 1 works of Cell 6 (3 culverts in fish habitat, groundwater lowering, monitoring until Jan. 2, 2034) — DFO Registry 26-HQUE-00140 ↗.

Fact 5 · ECCC Enforcement Position

ECCC states that its inspections did not establish reasonable grounds to believe that an offence under the statutes examined had been committed and that no enforcement action is currently warranted.

Fact 6 · DFO Follow-ups (2027 & 2029)

DFO requires a post-construction report, a fish passage monitoring protocol for the three culverts, culvert monitoring in 2027 and 2029, and a follow-up program for offsetting measures to be determined after assessing actual impacts.

6. My Analysis, Reply and Request to the CEC

In its October 16, 2026 response to the CEC (72-rsp_en.pdf), Canada asked the Secretariat to terminate SEM-26-003 by invoking the pending Petition 0546 process. Now that the ministerial responses have been delivered on November 10, 2026, I have transmitted them to the CEC Secretariat and requested the preparation of an independent Factual Record under Article 24.28(1) of the USMCA. The decision on next steps belongs exclusively to the CEC.
