OFFICIAL STATUS · CEC NOTIFICATION OF NOV 25, 2026 (89/PP) · MERITS RESPONSE ORDERED

CEC Secretariat rejects Canada's procedural objection and requests merits response without delay

In its 13-page Notification signed on November 25, 2026 (A24.27(2)(3)/SEM/26-003/89/PP), the CEC Secretariat concludes that OAG Environmental Petition No. 0546 does not constitute a pending judicial or administrative proceeding under CUSMA Article 24.27(4)(a) and has already concluded. Noting that the 60-day deadline has expired without information under Article 24.27(4)(b), the Secretariat continues its review and requests Canada's merits response as soon as possible.
