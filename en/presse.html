Transparence & Rigueur Éditoriale Accuracy Policy: Why are certain media outlets not listed here?
View the criteria
Avertissement formel : Any publication, interview, quote, event, or blog post not expressly listed on this official website must under no circumstances be considered a reliable or representative source of my work.

If media coverage is excluded from this official platform, it is because it fails to meet the standards of file integrity for one or more of the following formal reasons:

01
Absence d'approbation préalable

The content has not been authorized, reviewed or validated by William Guindon.

02
Déni du droit de parole

The media outlet failed to arrange any prior interviews, consult the author, or refuse a fair right of reply.

03
Cadrage biaisé ou réducteur

The journalistic angle betrays the reality of the approach (e.g.: purely anecdotal framing instead of factual and legal rigor).

04
Distortion of facts

The information has been modified, invented, or the comments have been distorted or taken out of context.

05
Âgisme et condescendance

Marked use of ageism or institutional paternalism, infantilizing a leading international legal and scientific procedure.

06
Erreurs factuelles non corrigées

The publication contains material deficiencies, erroneous figures or inaccurate legal statements that have never been corrected.

Rigueur et intégrité : This is neither censorship nor favoritism, but a strict matter of rigor and protection of the evidentiary record. Faced with environmental and legal issues of international scope, factual accuracy and respect for due process must prevail over sensationalism, ageism, or journalistic shortcuts.
