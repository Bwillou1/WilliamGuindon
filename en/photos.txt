# Photo Gallery
Authentic photos of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville (Great Blainville Peat Bog), the terrain and
the stages of the citizen file SEM-26-003.

Saturday June 13, 2026 at 9:49 p.m.: start of writing the revised reply for CCE (ACEUM).
Cross-analysis of sections 35 and 36(3) of the Fisheries Act on the prohibition of destroying fish
habitat and discharging harmful substances, cross-checking of heavy metal analysis data (cadmium)
and satellite mapping.

Observations and photographic documentation in the field: wildlife remains suggesting acts of
poaching or unauthorized shooting, and wild deposits observed under Hydro-Québec rights of way in
the Plan-Bouchard sector. Contact details transmitted to the Protection of Quebec Wildlife
(MELCCFP).

During this bike ride, I headed towards the intermunicipal office to listen to see if any trucks
were circulating. I didn't hear any coming from the cell 6 area. Additionally, I was able to take in
the beautiful view of the water reflecting the clouds and watch firefighters perform tests.

Fence marking access to what appears to be a former National Defense sector at Plan-Bouchard. The
site remains inaccessible (padlocks sealed with wax), although a breach is visible on the other side
of the perimeter. The area could still harbor white phosphorous munitions residue. No hydrological
impact study seems to have assessed whether the pumping envisaged in cell 6 risks lowering the local
water table, while white phosphorus ignites spontaneously upon simple contact with air when it is
exposed.

Authentic aerial photograph showing the immediate proximity between the preserved natural wetland
and the industrial landfill site.

Precious ecosystem sheltering vulnerable species and playing a role as a major carbon sink for the
northern crown of Montreal.

William Guindon in Royal Canadian Army Cadet uniform, recipient of the Best Recruit award (2935
Bois-des-Filion Cadet Corps).

Official map of the Ville de Blainville (City of Blainville) demarcating the 550.0 hectares of
municipal natural environments of the Grande Tourbière and the 55.8 hectares acquired via the Green
and Blue Network (CMM Phase II), framing the industrial processing and landfill enclave of Stablex.

Official photograph of the natural environment of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville accompanying
the indigenous memory and environmental reconciliation file.

Morning light and plant strata of the Blainville ombrotrophic bog, a wetland of major ecological
importance for the metropolitan region.
