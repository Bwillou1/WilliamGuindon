Ecological Integrity

The Grande Tourbière de Blainville constitutes the largest wooded wetland in the Lower Laurentians and a major carbon reservoir. The industrial enclave in the center has been operated by Stablex since 1983.

Folder CCE SEM-26-003

This cartographic coverage supports the evidentiary register of citizen communication relating to the effective application of environmental laws (Articles 24.27 and 24.28 of the ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement)).
