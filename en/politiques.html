Architecture of the Ethical and Legal Framework

In order to guarantee absolute clarity, flawless traceability and rigorous legal accessibility for citizens, researchers, media and international bodies, the normative framework is split into distinct and autonomous policies, supplemented by the legal conditions of use of the platform. Each document has its own official page with its timestamp and delimited scope.
