PART 05 — THE THREE QUESTIONS SUBMITTED TO THE LOBBYING COMMISSIONER

Addressed to Ms. Nancy Bélanger, Commissioner of Lobbying of Canada (410, avenue Laurier Ouest, bureau 810, Ottawa ON K1R 1B7 · [email protected]). Submitted as regulatory compliance matters, not as accusations.

Reminder: Section 10.4(1) of the Lobbying Act requires the Commissioner to carry out an investigation if she has reason to believe that it is necessary.

Question 1 Exactitude du champ « société mère » — art. 5(2)b) et 5(2)d)

The field states "Republic service", 63 Trade Dr Bldg #4, Massena NY. However, this address is an operational service center (Republic Services Environmental Solutions directory; same address as OP-TECH and NRC NY Environmental Services; NYDEC site report S645029). The parent company is Republic Services, Inc., listed on the NYSE: RSG, headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona — official release dated May 2, 2022; Corporations Canada file 759873-4 (exemption for belonging to a company listed on the stock exchange; director in Phoenix); ISED notices 1995-07 and 2022-05 (ultimate control United States; buyer: Republic Services, Inc., Phoenix).

Question 2 Adequacy of declared categories — s. 5(1)(a) and 5(2)(h)

Categories checked: legislative proposal/bill; policies or programs. Declared detail: “advise and inform… keep informed of the work on cell 6”. No relevant bills, regulations, policies, programs, grants, contributions or contracts are identified — and federal records do not contain any in the client's name.

Question 3 Obligation de déclaration interne — art. 7(1)

The client's general manager is designated as the client's representative; no internal lobbying declaration (legal entity) exists in the Register for Stablex Canada inc. Question submitted for statutory verification, without taking a position.
