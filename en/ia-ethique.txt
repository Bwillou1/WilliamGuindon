# Ethics and Supervision Policy for Artificial Intelligence
Rules for using AI tools, transparency towards readers and editorial responsibility

### Principle position & transversal scope of application
This policy is not limited to the management of the website: it governs all activities, research
procedures, drafting of appeals or submissions (including SEM-26-003), citizen correspondence, open
letters and speaking engagements by William Guindon. “No content intended for the public or an
official authority is produced without full human control, and any algorithmic assistance remains
under the exclusive human supervision of William and his parents. » AI is a work support tool, never
a source of truth nor an autonomous author. Responsibility for any approach or publication remains
entirely human.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs the responsible and ethical use of computer technologies and artificial
intelligence in the context of:
- documentary research and the organization of evidentiary data;
- the drafting of citizen submissions, briefs, press releases and open letters;
- electronic communications and public correspondence;
- tools and services deployed on the williamguindon.me site.

## 2. Terms and framework for using AI infrastructures

I provide strict and functional supervision of IT technologies. Artificial intelligence tools are
used exclusively as technical support instruments, documentary research and software assistance, in
order to compensate for the substantial asymmetry of resources in the analysis of environmental
files.

### 2.1 Documentary analysis and exploration of complex corpora
I use indexing, semantic search and data processing technologies (notably full-text search engines
and cutting-edge language models) to identify, categorize and cross-reference evidentiary passages
within voluminous documentary sets (hydrogeological studies, authorization orders, technical
reports). These tools only intervene in the localization of textual elements; the legal
qualification, the interpretation of the facts and the writing of theses remain under my exclusive
human appreciation.

### 2.2 Running local models for common tasks
For elementary linguistic operations that do not require any remote computing power — such as
syntactic revision, optimization of sentence structures and occasional textual corrections — I give
priority to the Bonsai 27B model (derived from the Qwen open source architecture), executed entirely
locally on my computer hardware. This protocol ensures the strict confidentiality of the data
processed and prevents any unnecessary transfer of requests to third-party servers.

### 2.3 Technical assistance for source code and web tools
I use models to assist in the development, debugging and standardization of programming files (HTML,
style sheets, functional scripts) hosted on my domain. Each generated software or algorithmic
component is subject to a functional audit and manual validation prior to its integration into
production.

### 2.4 Proportionality and environmental assessment
The energy consumption induced by the hosted calculations (estimated at a few tens of kilowatt hours
on the Hydro-Québec network, which is largely carbon-free) meets a criterion of material
proportionality. This recourse is limited to steps aimed at documenting and preventing permanent and
large-scale environmental damage (in particular the protection of wetlands and the control of
dangerous materials).

## 3. Prohibited uses

- Publish a text, image, sound or video substantially generated by generative AI without identifying
-   them as such (principle 14.7 of the Quebec Press Council).
- Present unverified AI output as verified fact (models can generate factual errors).
- Using AI to fabricate evidence, alter documents or create false testimonies.
- Transmit personal, confidential or sensitive information (sources, internal documents, data of
-   minors) to an AI model.
- Relying on AI for editorial decisions or to distort source statements.

## 4. Human editorial control — procedure

Before any publication or sending:
- The final content is written or edited by William from verified sources;
- Any AI output used is reread, cross-referenced with the sources and corrected by William;
- Facts, figures, quotes and documents are checked against the original sources, never against AI
-   output alone;
- Social media posts and emails prepared with AI assistance are always double-checked by William
-   before sending/publishing;
- Any published content substantially generated by AI is visibly and inseparably identified.

## 5. Transparency, attribution and primacy of human inquiry

Automated systems are by nature incapable of carrying out a legal investigation process, determining
an evidentiary strategy or understanding the material reality of the premises. The entire
investigation of my files is based on my personal investigation work:
- Archive examination and evidentiary conservation: I myself carry out the identification in the
-   public archives of historical documents, judgments or court reports not indexed by the usual
-   search engines, of which I ensure the manual capture, temporal authentication, classification and
-   inclusion in the proof annexes.
- Exercise of rights of access to information: I personally ensure the formulation, legal
-   qualification, formal filing and monitoring of requests for access to documents from public bodies
-   as well as related administrative appeals with provincial and federal authorities.
- Direct obtaining of technical documents: I obtained and assembled administrative correspondence,
-   specialized hydrogeological studies and engineering plans not publicly disseminated by direct
-   approaches to information custodians.
- Physical findings in the field: I conducted the in situ examination of topography, receiving
-   watercourses and threatened wetlands, while personally cross-checking these observations with
-   geospatial data and consulting with independent scientific experts.
- Counter-expertise and detection of anomalies: I myself identify methodological deviations,
-   toxicological anomalies and excesses of standards in industrial and government reports.
- Legal theory and formulation of appeals: I developed all of the legal argumentation in support of my
-   efforts (in particular under the mechanisms of ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico
-   Agreement)/CUSMA). AI does not intervene at any time in the design of the theses defended.
- Methodological transparency: My use of data processing technologies is fully explained. It
-   constitutes a method of technical organization proportionate to ensuring the exhaustive processing
-   of thousands of regulatory pages.
- Traceability and opposability by primary sources: The validity of my work is based on the probative
-   force of officially referenced official acts, engineering studies and legal provisions. No
-   assertion arises from autonomous algorithmic processing not corroborated by a verifiable material
-   source.
- Accountability and personal editorial responsibility: Any content made public or filed before a
-   court or an international organization engages my exclusive personal responsibility, without
-   intellectual delegation or release of liability.

## 6. Liability

The site remains solely editorially responsible for all published content, including if an error
comes from an AI tool. The use of an AI tool is not an excuse and does not waive the rules of
truthfulness and accuracy.

## 8. Absolute confidentiality and non-consultation of personal API keys (BYO-API)

### Formal commitment: Your API keys remain 100% local and private
William Guindon has no access to, does not store on any server, and never sees your API keys or
personal access tokens (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Mistral AI, OpenRouter, Hugging
Face, etc.).
- Storage exclusively confined to the user's terminal: Your API keys and local instance addresses
-   (Ollama, LM Studio) are saved exclusively within your own browser's local sandbox (multi-level
-   localStorage, IndexedDB and CacheStorage to resist session cookie dumps without ever leaving your
-   machine).
- Direct and encrypted client-provider connection: The requests made pass directly and without
-   intermediary from your browser to the official gateways of AI providers (e.g.
-   generativelanguage.googleapis.com, api.openai.com, api.anthropic.com, api.mistral.ai,
-   openrouter.ai). No proxy, mirror server or log managed by William Guindon intercepts, sees or logs
-   your identifiers or the content of your exchanges.
- Control and right to immediate oblivion: You can view your key in plain text at any time using the
-   display button (), modify it or irrevocably purge it from your browser via the “Forget” button.
