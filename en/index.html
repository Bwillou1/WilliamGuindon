Analyse contradictoire · SEM-26-003

Comparateur : Argument d'Ottawa (72-RSP) vs. Réalité Juridique & Preuves

1. Invocation of OAG Petition 0546 (Art. 24.27(4)(a))
Ottawa's Claim (Oct. 16): Citizen petition No. 0546 filed by the author with the Auditor General constitutes a 'pending proceeding' warranting closing or staying the CEC file.
Legal Reality (Oct. 17): In law (s. 22 AG Act) and established CEC jurisprudence, an OAG petition is an informational parliamentary mechanism without coercive or sanction power, not a judicial or quasi-judicial proceeding initiated by the government.
2. Cadmium Contamination (Fisheries Act, s. 36(3))
Ottawa's Claim (Oct. 16): Complete silence. No counter-expert testimony, no federal inspection report, and no scientific rebuttal produced for Cell 6.
Evidence on Record: Cadmium discharges measured up to 320 times aquatic life criteria, certified spill of 1,300 L (Bureau Veritas), and documented fish mortality. Undisputed material fact.
3. Destruction of 278,000 m² (BAPE 371 & Bill 93)
Ottawa's Claim (Oct. 16): No federal ecological justification and no prior statutory authorization under the Species at Risk Act or Fisheries Act is presented.
Evidence on Record: Report BAPE 371 recommended environmental rejection of the project, which was bypassed by Bill 93 passed under gag order without federal impact assessment.
4. Guideline 7.5 & Recommendation for a Factual Record
Ottawa's Claim (Oct. 16): Asserts that awaiting the ministerial reply of November 10, 2026 would prevent 'duplication' of procedures.
Legal Reality (Oct. 17): The CEC determination of August 17 (paras. 66-79) already affirmed that remedies were exhausted. The Secretariat is currently reviewing the file under CUSMA Art. 24.28 (60 working days).
5. OPPORTUNITY TO ENGAGE VS PRIVATE AUDIENCE (LOBBYING REGISTRY)
Ottawa's Claim (Oct. 16): Claims the submitter did not 'provide an opportunity to communicate the requested information' as justification for dismissal.
Evidence on Record (Oct. 24): While Environment and Climate Change Canada kept silent for 119 days on the 10 citizen inquiries (ECCC receipt No. PIC-20260615-082854), the federal lobbying registry certifies a bilateral meeting on September 17, 2026 between the Associate Assistant Deputy Minister and Stablex's lobbyist specifically addressing Cell 6 (Report No. 385380-701620). Documented material fact proving access asymmetry.

October 17, 2026: Submitter legal rebuttal refuting Canada's motion to dismiss

Less than 24 hours after Canada's response, William Guindon filed a formal brief of Observations detailing 7 legal grounds with CEC Legal Director Paolo Solano:

  • Inapplicability of Art. 24.27(4)(a): Petition 0546 to the Auditor General is neither a judicial nor administrative proceeding initiated by the Party, nor quasi-judicial (no sanctioning power or verification of answers by the OAG).
  • Guideline 7.5 is protective, not an escape clause: It aims to protect the submitter's private remedies, not to provide an escape hatch for the Party.
  • Matter already decided by the Secretariat: Paragraphs 66-79 of the August 17, 2026 determination (DET-26-003) already ruled on the coexistence of proceedings and reasonable time.
  • Difference in nature: CEC review = enforcement of 17 provisions across 5 federal statutes vs OAG petition = 24 questions without allegation of failure to enforce.
  • Disproportionate burden: Contrary to CEC practice and protective standards for minor citizens acting alone (precedents Loggerhead Turtle, Fairview Terminal).
  • Report indéterminé : Le délai du 10 novembre peut être prorogé unilatéralement par les ministres en vertu de l'art. 22 de la Loi sur le vérificateur général.
  • Zéro mesure d'application sur le fond : Le Canada ne démontre aucune mesure d'application fédérale pour la cellule 6 (par. 54, 64, 76).

1. What Canada's official response contains

The federal government's brief centers its defense on a procedural motion to dismiss:

  • Request for termination or stay: Canada formally asks the Secretariat to terminate submission SEM-26-003, in whole or in part, or stay proceedings.
  • Duplication claim (OAG Petition 0546): Canada argues that the international proceeding is 'premature' because submitter William Guindon also filed Environmental Petition No. 0546 with the Auditor General of Canada (CESD). The federal ministers have a 120-day statutory response deadline expiring on November 10, 2026.
  • Invocation of Guideline 7.5: Canada claims that continuing international review prior to the national petition reply would create procedural overlap.

2. What Canada concedes on the scientific merits

The Government of Canada does not refute any of the substantive environmental and scientific findings established in the citizen dossier:

  • No proof of federal inspection, investigation, or prior statutory permit issued for Cell 6 is produced in Canada's response.
  • Cadmium discharges measured up to 320 times aquatic life criteria are completely unrefuted by the government.

3. Next legal steps (Review under CUSMA Article 24.28)

In his official acknowledgment of October 16, 2026, CEC Legal Director Paolo Solano confirmed that “the Secretariat will review the notification transmitted by the Party pursuant to paragraph 24.27(4)(a) of CUSMA”:

  • Le Secrétariat détermine si la simple existence d'une pétition d'information au Vérificateur général (qui ne constitue pas une action judiciaire exécutoire) justifie ou non de surseoir à l'instruction du dossier international.
  • Si les explications du Canada ne dissipent pas les allégations de défaut d'application de la Loi sur les pêches et de la LEP, le Secrétariat recommandera formellement aux ministres de l'Environnement du Canada, des États-Unis et du Mexique l'ouverture d'un Dossier factuel indépendant.

The Stablex file

A thousand-year-old ecosystem facing the landfill of hazardous waste

Activist dedicated to ecosystem protection and the rights of future generations. At 15, he leads the historic Grande Tourbière de Blainville dossier before the Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC SEM-26-003) and international bodies to demand transparency and defend our shared future.

  • September 2023 : The Bureau d'audiences publiques sur l'environnement (BAPE) concludes that the expansion project (Cell 6) is “premature”.
  • December 2023 : A citizen sample reveals a concentration of cadmium measured at 0.016 mg/L in nearby surface waters, up to 320 times the water quality criterion for the protection of aquatic life (acute toxicity, MELCCFP (Québec Ministry of the Environment, the Fight against Climate Change, Wildlife and Parks)).
  • March 2025 : The Quebec government adopts Projet de loi 93 under gag order, forcing the transfer of land belonging to Ville de Blainville (City of Blainville), including 278,000 m² of wetlands, to allow the expansion of the site.
  • Blocked appeals : The law contains clauses excluding any legal appeal on the merits of the decision, which the Centre québécois du droit de l'environnement (CQDE) describes as a serious violation of the right to an effective remedy.
  • Imports : Approximately 41% of the materials buried in Blainville come from outside Quebec, including approximately 29% from the United States, which establishes the cross-border dimension of the appeal filed with the CCE.

It is in this context that William Guindon chooses the path of law: rather than confrontation, he mobilizes national and international mechanisms: BAPE, ACEUM, UN, to demand that Canada and Quebec respect their own environmental obligations.

Who is William Guindon?

William Guindon (born in 2011 in Quebec) is a Quebec environmental activist and student in the Laurentians. Author of the communication SEM-26-003 filed at the age of 14, he became at 15 the first minor in the history of Environmental Submissions (SEM) to obtain a positive determination of admissibility before the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE / ACEUM). Consult his detailed biography, the Stablex chronology or the press sheet. ↗ ↗ ↗

What is the SEM-26-003 file with CCE?

Communication SEM-26-003 (“Landfilling of hazardous materials in Blainville”) denounces the failure to apply Canadian federal environmental laws (migratory birds, species at risk, cadmium discharge) in the context of the expansion of the Stablex site into 278,000 m² of wetlands. Find the complete chronology of the file. ↗

Why is the Grande Tourbière de Blainville threatened?

Activist dedicated to ecosystem protection and the rights of future generations. At 15, he leads the historic Grande Tourbière de Blainville dossier before the Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC SEM-26-003) and international bodies to demand transparency and defend our shared future. ↗

Has William Guindon received the official response from the Government of Canada?

Yes. On October 16, 2026, the Government of Canada formally filed its official response (Exhibit 72-RSP) with the CCE Secretariat. William Guindon received notification of this and filed on October 17, 2026 a memorandum of observations in 7 legal grounds refuting Ottawa's attempt to avoid and requesting the creation of an independent factual file.

What are the next steps following the submission of Canada's response?

Following the submission of Canada's response on October 16, 2026 and citizen submissions on October 17, 2026, the CCE Secretariat is reviewing the file under section 24.28 of ACEUM to determine whether it recommends the development of an independent Factual File subject to a vote by the Trinational Council of Ministers.
