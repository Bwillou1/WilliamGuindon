File CCE SEM-26-003 & William Guindon

William Guindon (15 years old) is the author of the international citizen communication SEM-26-003 filed with the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE / ACEUM (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement)). This approach aims to enforce environmental protection laws and block the expansion of the Stablex hazardous waste landfill site in the heart of Grande Tourbière de Blainville.

International procedure in progress

Following the official response from the Government of Canada (Oct. 16, 2026) and the author's comments (Oct. 17, 2026), the CCE (Commission for Environmental Cooperation) Secretariat is considering the recommendation for an independent factual record.

Major environmental risk

Documented alerts of cadmium contamination and leaching reaching up to 320× standards, threatening the peatland and surrounding drinking water sources.

Policies, Ethics & Anti-SLAPP

100% independent citizen approach, protection against SLAPP lawsuits (anti-SLAPP), zero corporate funding and strict evidentiary rigor.

About William Guindon

Early commitment, uncompromising determination

William Guindon (born August 3, 2011 in Sainte-Marthe-sur-le-Lac, Quebec) is a Quebec environmental activist recognized for his commitment to ecosystem protection and the rights of future generations. Author at age 14 of the official submission filed with the Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC) under CUSMA and of a brief to the UN, he became at age 15 the first minor in history to obtain a positive CEC determination in the case of the Grande Tourbière de Blainville.

Faced with Bill 93 passed under gag order and cadmium discharges measured up to 320 times acute aquatic toxicity criteria, he strictly chooses the rule of law and evidentiary transparency, supported by the pro bono advice of sixteen independent scientists and jurists.

Key actions

Highlights: from Blainville to the United Nations

  • September 25, 2026 Official CEC Confirmation: First minor in the history of the SEM process (since 1994) The Submissions on Enforcement Matters (SEM) Unit of the Secretariat of the Commission for Environmental Cooperation formally confirms in writing: “since 1994, this is the first time a submitter has indicated to us that they were a minor at the time of filing a submission with the CEC”, attesting to the unprecedented historical nature of the citizen proceeding led by William Guindon.
  • August 17, 2026 Positive CEC Determination — Canada's response required by October 16, 2026 Le Secrétariat de la CCE rend sa détermination officielle (PDF) ↗ en vertu des articles 24.27(2) et (3) de l'ACEUM, jugeant que la communication satisfaisait aux critères et demandant une réponse au gouvernement du Canada d'ici le October 16, 2026. William Guindon marque l'histoire en devenant le premier mineur à déposer et réussir à obtenir une décision positive dans le registre mondial des Soumissions SEM.
  • November 28, 2021 Among the first children vaccinated against COVID-19 in Quebec At age ten, William Guindon was one of the very first Quebec children in the 5-to-11 age group to receive the COVID-19 vaccine. His initiative earned him public congratulations from the Premier of Quebec, François Legault, reported by the Journal de Montréal.
  • April 8, 2025 Open letter in Le Devoir: “François Legault, you are destroying our future” He published an open letter in the daily Le Devoir, directly challenging the head of the Quebec government on the environmental and intergenerational consequences of the Stablex facility expansion project, authorized by Bill 93 passed under gag order despite the unfavorable report of the BAPE.
  • May 1, 2026 International Submission SEM-26-003 (CEC / CUSMA) He officially filed submission SEM-26-003 “Hazardous Waste Disposal in Blainville” with the Commission for Environmental Cooperation, the trinational body established under the Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement. He contends that Canada is failing to effectively enforce its federal laws protecting migratory birds, species at risk, and water quality. The CEC acknowledged receipt on May 4, 2026 and initiated its review under CUSMA Articles 24.27(2) and (3).
  • May 2026 Formal Deposition to the United Nations Special Rapporteur Il transmet au Dr Marcos A. Orellana, Rapporteur spécial de l'ONU sur les substances toxiques et les droits de l'homme, le mémoire « Formal Deposition and Urgent Appeal: Human Rights Violations and Denial of Justice – The Stablex Case and the Imposition of Bill 93 in Quebec » ↗, au nom de la communauté de Blainville et à titre de représentant des générations futures. Le document soutient que le Projet de loi 93 viole l'article 7 de la Charte canadienne des droits et libertés, l'article 3 de la Déclaration universelle des droits de l'homme et la résolution 76/300 de l'Assemblée générale de l'ONU reconnaissant le droit à un environnement sain.
  • June 2026 Environmental Petition No. 546 (Auditor General of Canada / CESD) He formally submitted Environmental Petition No. 15-546 ↗ to the Commissioner of the Environment and Sustainable Development (Office of the Auditor General of Canada), triggering the statutory obligation for federal departments (ECCC, DFO, Health Canada) to formally respond regarding federal law enforcement (Fisheries Act, SARA, MBCA) in Blainville.

The Stablex file

A thousand-year-old ecosystem facing the landfill of hazardous waste

The Grande Tourbière de Blainville is a thousand-year-old ecosystem located in Blainville, included by the Quebec Ministry of the Environment in the Atlas of Territories of Interest for Conservation in the St. Lawrence Lowlands. The Stablex site, in operation since 1983, is the only center authorized in Quebec for the final burial of materials resulting from the treatment by stabilization and solidification of inorganic industrial residues.

  • September 2023: The Bureau d'audiences publiques sur l'environnement (BAPE) concludes that the expansion project (Cell 6) is “premature”.
  • December 2023: A citizen sample reveals a concentration of cadmium measured at 0.016 mg/L in nearby surface waters, up to 320 times the water quality criterion for the protection of aquatic life (acute toxicity, MELCCFP (Québec Ministry of the Environment, the Fight against Climate Change, Wildlife and Parks)).
  • March 2025: The Quebec government adopts Projet de loi 93 under gag order, forcing the transfer of land belonging to Ville de Blainville (City of Blainville), including 278,000 m² of wetlands, to allow the expansion of the site.
  • Blocked appeals: The law contains clauses excluding any legal appeal on the merits of the decision, which the Centre québécois du droit de l'environnement (CQDE) describes as a serious violation of the right to an effective remedy.
  • Imports: Approximately 41% of the materials buried in Blainville come from outside Quebec, including approximately 29% from the United States, which establishes the cross-border dimension of the appeal filed with the CCE.

It is in this context that William Guindon chooses the path of law: rather than confrontation, he mobilizes national and international mechanisms: BAPE, ACEUM, UN, to demand that Canada and Quebec respect their own environmental obligations.

Who is William Guindon?

William Guindon (born in 2011 in Quebec) is a Quebec environmental activist and student in the Laurentians. Author of the communication SEM-26-003 filed at the age of 14, he became at 15 the first minor in the history of Environmental Submissions (SEM) to obtain a positive determination of admissibility before the Commission de coopération environnementale (CCE / ACEUM). Consult his detailed biography, the Stablex chronology or the press sheet. ↗ ↗ ↗

What is the SEM-26-003 file with CCE?

Communication SEM-26-003 (“Landfilling of hazardous materials in Blainville”) denounces the failure to apply Canadian federal environmental laws (migratory birds, species at risk, cadmium discharge) in the context of the expansion of the Stablex site into 278,000 m² of wetlands. Find the complete chronology of the file. ↗

Why is Grande Tourbière de Blainville threatened?

Activist dedicated to ecosystem protection and the rights of future generations. At 15, he leads the historic Grande Tourbière de Blainville dossier before the Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC SEM-26-003) and international bodies to demand transparency and defend our shared future. ↗

Has William Guindon received the official response from the Government of Canada?

Yes. On October 16, 2026, the Government of Canada formally filed its official response (Exhibit 72-RSP) with the CCE Secretariat. William Guindon received notification of this and filed on October 17, 2026 a memorandum of observations in 7 legal grounds refuting Ottawa's attempt to avoid and requesting the creation of an independent factual file.

What are the next steps following the submission of Canada's response?

Following the submission of Canada's response on October 16, 2026 and citizen submissions on October 17, 2026, the CCE Secretariat is reviewing the file under section 24.28 of ACEUM to determine whether it recommends the development of an independent Factual File subject to a vote by the Trinational Council of Ministers.
