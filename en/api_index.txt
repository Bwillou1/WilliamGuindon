# Open Public API & Machine Registry
Standardized, direct and keyless access point for language models (LLMs), researchers, developers
and autonomous agents wishing to query the entire textual corpus, official documents and 222
evidentiary documents in the SEM-26-003 file.

## Main Access Points (REST & Open Data Endpoints)

### Complete Corpus Full Text
Returns the raw and structured text of all the pages of the site, the 12 fundamental charters,
ecological analyses, the citizen survey and governance metadata (+46,000 words).

### Hugging Face Dataset (AI & LLMs)
Official dataset structured and ready for machine learning, fine-tuning, benchmarks and RAG
pipelines, distributed on the Hugging Face hub.

### Directory of 222 Probative Documents
Exhaustive index of the 222 items in the SEM-26-003 archive: BAPE 371 reports, GIS maps, Sentinel-2
NDVI satellite data, well tests and matches with their Internet Archive URLs and SHA-256 hashes.

### Legal Status & Deadline CCE
Live status of the SEM-26-003 submission to the CCE (ACEUM) Secretariat, compliance indicators and
countdown to the October 16, 2026 deadline.

### Text Feeds Dedicated to LLMs
Files compliant with llmstxt.org standards injectable directly into the context windows of AI models
(GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Llama 3).
